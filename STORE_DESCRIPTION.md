## Short description

Play poker hands for chips and mult, hire jesters, and make a dark court laugh before it loses patience. A deckbuilding roguelike where your head is the ante.

## About This Game

You are the fool. The court is bored, dangerous, and watching.

Jester is a poker-scoring roguelike set in a baroque masquerade court. Play poker hands, multiply chips by mult, and reach each round's target before your hands run out. Survive eight venues, from the Scullery up to the Throne Room, and win the favor of the King. Fail, and the court loses interest in you.

**Build an act that breaks the scoreboard**
- Hire from 61 jesters across three rarities. Some add flat chips or mult, some multiply it, some scale as the run goes on, and some copy the jester beside them.
- Order matters. Jesters score left to right, so drag them around and find the arrangement that explodes.
- Watch it land. Every card and jester pops in turn, the counters heat up as the hand outgrows the target, and the total rolls into the score while the King's portrait reacts.

**Rewrite your deck**
- Masquerade masks level up each poker hand, with a commedia stock character for every hand.
- Decrees permanently change your deck: Bonus and Mult cards, Wild cards that count as every suit, Glass cards that hit for X2 but can shatter, and cards that raise a rank, set a suit, or destroy cards outright.
- Permanent props add a hand, a discard, a jester slot, or a mask slot, but you can only buy one per ante.

**Face the courtiers**
- The last round of every ante is a boss. The Executioner allows a single hand, the Censor takes your discards, the Poet Laureate bans repeating a hand type, the Tax Collector takes $1 for every hand you play, and the Spymaster silences your leftmost jester.
- The final round of ante 8 is always the King.

**Spend, sell, gamble**
- The shop opens after every round. Save money to earn interest, reroll the stock, sell jesters you've outgrown, and open packs for a pick of three.

**Features**
- Standard 52-card deck, 8-card hand, and up to 5 cards per play, with plain poker hand names.
- Cards drawn as aged ivory stock with split court-card panels and candlelit backdrops.
- Drag to arrange your hand, your played cards, and your jesters.
- Your run saves as you play and resumes where you left it, in the shop or mid-round.
- Built for landscape, from a wide desktop layout to a compact phone layout.

A short, replayable run you can finish in one sitting, or keep restarting until the court finally laughs.
